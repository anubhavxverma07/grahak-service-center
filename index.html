FAQ

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Jo har naya partner poochta hai — sab jawab yahin.

Registration ke liye kya documents chahiye?
Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank passbook (front page) aur ek passport size photo. Sab documents clear aur valid hone chahiye. Ek baar verify hone ke baad dobara nahi maangte.
Registration fees kitni hai?
Bilkul free. Koi registration fee, koi setup cost, koi monthly charges nahi. Aap sirf tab kamate hain jab aapke customer transaction karte hain.
Commission kitna milta hai?
Har service ka commission alag hai — recharge par 1-3%, AEPS par per transaction, bill payments par per bill. Detailed commission chart aapko activation ke baad dashboard mein milega.
Payout kab milta hai?
Daily payout. Aapke wallet mein commission turant add hoti hai, aur aap use apne bank account mein transfer kar sakte hain — usually usi din.
KYC verification mein kitna time lagta hai?
Usually 24 ghante. Agar documents clear hain toh kuch cases mein same-day approval bhi mil jata hai. Verification complete hone par SMS aur email aata hai.
Kya main mobile se bhi use kar sakta hoon?
Haan. Platform mobile, tablet aur computer — sab par kaam karta hai. Android app bhi available hai Play Store par.
Support kaise milega?
24×7 WhatsApp support, phone support (9 AM - 9 PM) aur email. Hindi, English aur regional languages mein support available hai.
Kya transactions live hain?
Haan. Hamare saare services RBI-compliant banking partners ke through live chalti hain. Real money, real transactions, real commissions.
