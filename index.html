FAQ

Questions, answered.

What services are available?

Core services include AEPS, BBPS, money transfer, recharge, PAN card and DTH services. Exact availability depends on the partner setup and authorized service providers.

Can I register as a partner online?

You can submit the partner registration form on this website. Final onboarding, verification and activation require the appropriate backend process.

Are transactions live on this website?

This frontend is prepared for integration. Real financial transactions require authorized APIs, credentials, compliance checks and a connected backend.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The website is responsive and designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
