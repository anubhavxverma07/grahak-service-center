To become a partner on our platform, there is a one-time ₹1,499 registration fee. This includes:

  • KYC verification and documentation
  • Lifetime dashboard access
  • Free training and onboarding support
  • 24×7 WhatsApp support
  • Activation of all services

Additional Charges

After registration, some services require you to load money into your wallet. These charges are separate and are used for your own transactions — they don't go to us:

Commission Structure

Each service has a different commission. Detailed chart is provided in your dashboard after activation:

  • AEPS: Per transaction commission
  • Railway Ticket: ₹20–40 per ticket
  • Recharge: 1–3% per transaction
  • BBPS Bills: ₹5–15 per bill
  • Money Transfer: 0.5–1% per transaction
  • Insurance: 10–20% of premium

Payout Policy

  • Commission credited instantly to your wallet
  • Bank transfer same-day or next-day
  • Minimum payout: ₹100
  • Payout charges: Nil

Important Terms

  • Registration fee is non-refundable once KYC is approved
  • Minimum wallet balance of ₹500 must be maintained to keep services active
  • Fake documents or incorrect information may lead to account suspension
  • Commission rates may change per company policy — with 30 days prior notice
  • In case of disputes, the company's decision is final

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything a new partner wants to know — answered here.

What documents are needed for registration?
Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank passbook (front page) and one passport-size photo. All documents must be clear and valid. Once verified, we don't ask for them again.
What is the registration fee? Are there hidden charges?
The registration fee is ₹1,499 — one-time. After that, you need to load a minimum of ₹500 into your wallet (which is your own money, used for transactions). No hidden charges. Full pricing details are on the Pricing page.
Is AEPS available?
Yes! AEPS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System) is one of our core services. You can offer cash withdrawal, balance enquiry, mini statement and Aadhaar-to-Aadhaar transfer — all using the customer's fingerprint.
Is railway ticket booking available?
Yes! Railway ticket booking is our newest service. You can book train tickets, check PNR status and handle cancellations and refunds — all from your counter.
How much commission do I earn?
Each service has a different commission — AEPS pays per transaction, recharge pays 1-3%, railway tickets pay ₹20-40, bill payments pay per bill, money transfer pays 0.5-1%. Detailed chart is provided in your dashboard after activation.
When do I receive my payout?
Daily payout. Commission is credited instantly to your wallet, and you can transfer it to your bank account — usually the same day.
How long does KYC verification take?
Usually 24 hours. If documents are clear, some cases get same-day approval. You'll receive an SMS and email once verification is complete.
Can I use it from my mobile?
Yes. The platform works on mobile, tablet and computer. An Android app is also available on Play Store.
How do I get support?
24×7 WhatsApp support, phone support (10 AM – 7 PM) and email. Support is available in Hindi, English and regional languages.
Are transactions live?
Yes. All our services run live through RBI-regulated banking partners. Real money, real transactions, real commissions.
